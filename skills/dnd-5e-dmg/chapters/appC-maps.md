# Appendix C: Maps

## Core Idea
A short image-only appendix of sample dungeon/building maps (multi-floor building, several dungeon layouts, all at "1 square = 5 feet" scale) provided as ready-to-reuse or inspiration material — the book's own advice here is to not spend DM prep time drawing maps from scratch when reusable ones already exist.

## Key Concepts
- **Repurposing existing maps** — the chapter's explicit guidance: unless you have something very specific in mind, conserve prep time by reusing a published map (from this appendix, other published adventures, or online sources) rather than drawing one from scratch.
- **Standard scale** — all sample maps here use 1 square = 5 feet, matching the standard 5e grid/miniatures scale (Ch 8's tactical map rules).

## Mental Models
- Treat map-drawing time as a scarce resource — reserve custom cartography for locations that are genuinely plot-critical or narratively unique; reuse or lightly reskin existing maps (change room labels/contents, keep the geometry) for everything else.

## Connects To
- **Ch 5**: dungeon feature guidance (walls, doors, features) informs how to read/reuse/dress these sample maps.
- **Ch 8**: the 1 square = 5 feet scale matches the tactical map and miniatures rules.
- **Appendix A**: the Random Dungeons generator is the alternative to using a pre-made map when you want a fresh layout instead of an existing one.
