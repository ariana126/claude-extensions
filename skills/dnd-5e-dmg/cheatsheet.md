# DMG Cheatsheet

## Setting a DC
- Very Easy 5 · Easy 10 · Moderate 15 · Hard 20 · Very Hard 25 · Nearly Impossible 30.
- If you only ever use 10/15/20, the game runs fine. Don't roll for DC 5 — just let it succeed.
- DC 25 is fair past ~10th level; DC 30 is brutal even for a maxed 20th-level specialist (needs a 19-20).

## Building a Combat Encounter
1. Sum party's Easy/Medium/Hard/Deadly XP thresholds (by character level).
2. Sum monster XP, multiply by count-based multiplier (×1 solo, ×1.5 pair, ×2 for 3-6, ×2.5 for 7-10, ×3 for 11-14, ×4 for 15+; shift one tier for parties <3 or 6+).
3. Compare adjusted XP to party thresholds → that's your difficulty.
- **Default day budget**: 6-8 medium/hard encounters, 2 short rests (~1/3 and 2/3 through the day).
- **Watch for CR traps**: a monster's CR can understate real danger at low levels (single big hits, high-level-only immunities) — sanity check, don't trust the math blindly.

## Treasure & Magic Items
| Rarity | Level | Value | Max spell lvl (item) | Max static bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1st+ | 50-100 gp | 1st | — |
| Uncommon | 1st+ | 101-500 gp | 3rd | +1 |
| Rare | 5th+ | 501-5,000 gp | 6th | +2 |
| Very rare | 11th+ | 5,001-50,000 gp | 8th | +3 |
| Legendary | 17th+ | 50,001+ gp | 9th | +4 |
- Attunement cap: **3 items per creature**, always.
- Require attunement if passing the item around would be disruptive, or it stacks with a common bonus type.
- Rarity is a guideline, not a rule — break it for story when it's worth a good story.

## Damage Severity (Setback / Dangerous / Deadly) — use for traps, hazards, AND improvised damage
| Level | Setback | Dangerous | Deadly |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | 1d10 | 2d10 | 4d10 |
| 5-10 | 2d10 | 4d10 | 10d10 |
| 11-16 | 4d10 | 10d10 | 18d10 |
| 17-20 | 10d10 | 18d10 | 24d10 |
- Trap save DC bands: Setback 10-11 · Dangerous 12-15 · Deadly 16-20 (attack bonus +3/+6/+9 to +5/+8/+12 respectively).
- This is ONE table — memorize it once, use it for every "how much should this hurt" question.

## Monster Design Quick Reference (by CR — sample)
| CR | AC | HP | Attack | Dmg/Rd | Save DC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 13 | 71-85 | +3 | 9-14 | 13 |
| 5 | 15 | 131-145 | +6 | 33-38 | 15 |
| 10 | 17 | 206-220 | +7 | 63-68 | 16 |
| 15 | 18 | 281-295 | +8 | 93-98 | 18 |
| 20 | 19 | 356-400 | +10 | 123-140 | 19 |
- Final CR = average of (defensive CR from HP, adjusted ±1 per 2 pts AC deviates) and (offensive CR from dmg/round, adjusted ±1 per 2 pts attack bonus deviates). Round to nearest CR.
- A single monster at party level = fair fight for 4 PCs. Lower CR needed if fought in groups.

## Leveling & Advancement — pick ONE model per campaign
| Model | Use when |
|---|---|
| Combat XP | Crunchy, combat-heavy games |
| Noncombat challenge XP | Want to reward risky social/exploration play too |
| Milestones | Story-paced; award XP as easy(minor)/hard(major) beat-equivalents, or free rests/Hit Dice/charges |
| Session-based | 2nd lvl after session 1, 3rd after another, 4th after 2 more, then ~2-3 sessions/level |
| Story-based | Level at narrative goals, DM's call |

## Rest Pacing — pick ONE tier deliberately
| Tier | Short | Long | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1 hr | 8 hr | Baseline heroic pace |
| Epic Heroism | 5 min | 1 hr | Combat routine — compensate with harder fights |
| Gritty Realism | 8 hr | 7 days | Forces caution, favors intrigue/politics campaigns |

## World-Shaking Events
- **Budget ~3 per campaign**: one near the beginning, middle, end. More = "false action," stops landing.
- Small-scale local events (town/village-level) can happen as often as you like.

## Social Interaction — DC needed by attitude
| Attitude | No risk | Minor risk | Major risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friendly | Auto (DC 0) | DC 10 | DC 20 |
| Indifferent | DC 0 (still no harm) | DC 10 | DC 20 (minor risk only) |
| Hostile | Actively opposes | DC 10 (no harm) | DC 20 (no risk only) |
- Attitude shifts **at most 1 step** per conversation. Touching a known Ideal/Bond/Flaw is how you shift it.
- Failing an Insight check (by 10+) to learn a trait risks learning a FALSE/inverted one.

## Downtime Quick Costs
| Activity | Time | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Sow a rumor | Village 2d6 / Town 4d6 / City 6d6 days (continuous) | DC 15 CHA |
| Sell a magic item | 1d4-1d10 days to find buyer (by rarity) | DC 20 INT (Investigation), then CHA (Persuasion) roll |
| Craft magic item | 25 gp of value/day | Level min by rarity (3rd/3rd/6th/11th/17th) |
| Gain 1 renown | current renown × 10 days | — |
| Train to next level (variant) | 10-40 days by level band | 20-80 gp |

## Tells & Smells
- **If a random result feels absurd** (tiny room = temple, huge room = closet): override it. The system expects this.
- **If an encounter's adjusted XP > 1/3 of the day's total budget AND it's multi-wave**: it'll play harder than the math says — no short rest between waves.
- **If a player fails a check by exactly 1-2**: consider Success at a Cost instead of a flat fail.
- **If a player fails by 5+**: consider a worse failure than a narrow miss (Degrees of Failure).
- **If a cursed item's curse would be found by identify/short-rest study**: don't allow it — curses must surprise on use.
- **If you're about to give a low-level party a single monster with CR far above their level**: check for save-based abilities/immunities that could trivialize their spellcasters before running it.
- **If a downtime rumor campaign gets interrupted by an adventure**: it resets — days must be continuous in town/city.
