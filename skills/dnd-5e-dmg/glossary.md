# Glossary

**Adventuring Day** — the XP budget a party can handle before needing a long rest; ~6-8 medium/hard encounters (Ch 3, Ch 8).

**Artifact** — a unique, campaign-defining magic item with bespoke beneficial/detrimental properties and a specific destruction method; not found via random treasure tables (Ch 7).

**Attunement** — the bond a creature must form (via a focused short rest) to use certain items' magic; capped at 3 items per creature (Ch 7).

**Being Chase** (Chase) — a structured pursuit subsystem using Dash-limited action economy and a Stealth-vs-passive-Perception escape check (Ch 8).

**Bond** (NPC) — one of the Ten Sentences traits; a person/place/thing the NPC cares about, exploitable in social interaction once discovered (Ch 4).

**Carousing** — a downtime activity spent living it up; resolved via d100+level table (Ch 6).

**Challenge Rating (CR)** — a monster's power benchmark, derived by averaging independently-computed defensive and offensive ratings (Ch 9).

**Chamber Contents / Purpose** — the two-step process (what's in a room, why the room exists) for stocking a dungeon (Ch 5, Appendix A).

**Cheat sheet term**: **Cursed Item** — a magic item whose curse resists identification and can't be voluntarily un-attuned without breaking the curse first (Ch 7).

**Damage Severity by Level** — the shared Setback/Dangerous/Deadly × character-level table used for traps, hazards, and improvised damage everywhere in the book (Ch 5, Ch 8).

**Dawn War Deities** — the sample default pantheon (from the 4e Player's Handbook) reused as an example loose pantheon (Ch 1).

**Downtime Activity** — a between-adventures pursuit (crafting, carousing, training, business, rumors, renown, etc.) following a days→check→d100-table resolution pattern (Ch 6).

**Encounter Multiplier** — the XP-adjustment factor applied to multi-monster encounters based on monster count (Ch 3).

**Epic Boon** — one of 24 capstone rewards offered instead of further leveling past 20th level (Ch 7).

**Faction** (Dungeon) — a group of intelligent inhabitants competing for space/resources in a dungeon; multi-faction dungeons enable non-combat solutions (Ch 5).

**Flaw/Secret** (NPC) — the tenth Ten Sentences trait; an exploitable weakness or hidden shame (Ch 4).

**Gate-town** — one of 16 Outlands settlements each built around a permanent portal to one Outer Plane (Ch 2).

**Great Wheel / World Tree / World Axis** — three named cosmological models for arranging the planes (Ch 2).

**Hoard** (Treasure Hoard table) — the CR-keyed random table for a group's/lair's accumulated wealth, as opposed to Individual Treasure (Ch 7).

**Honor Score** — optional 7th ability score for code-of-conduct-driven campaigns (Ch 9).

**Ideal** (NPC) — one of the Ten Sentences traits; the value/principle an NPC holds, connectable to alignment (Ch 4).

**Individual Treasure** — the CR-keyed table for a single creature's pocket change (Ch 7).

**Inspiration** — a one-at-a-time resource granting advantage on one roll, awarded per the DM's chosen philosophy (roleplaying/heroism/victory/genre) (Ch 8).

**Loyalty Score** — a hidden 0-20 tracker of an NPC companion's devotion to the party (Ch 4).

**Madness (short/long-term/indefinite)** — escalating tiers of mental-break effects, resisted with WIS/CHA (or Sanity) saves (Ch 8).

**Magic Item Rarity** — the 5-tier common→legendary scale gating level-appropriateness and price (Ch 7).

**Milestone** — a designated story beat awarded XP (as an easy/hard encounter equivalent) or a small bonus reward instead of/alongside combat XP (Ch 8).

**Moral Quandary** — a forced values-tradeoff choice (Ally/Friend/Honor/Rescue/Respect) with no combat solution (Ch 3).

**Multiverse** — the full collection of planes of existence beyond the Material Plane (Ch 2).

**NPC (Nonplayer Character)** — any character controlled by the DM; depth of characterization scales with narrative weight (Ch 4).

**Optional Rule (Outer Plane)** — a named mechanical flavor effect unique to each of the 16 Outer Planes (Ch 2).

**Piety** — the Renown system reskinned for standing with a deity (Ch 1).

**Renown** — a per-organization 0+ standing score, raised by advancing/directly serving the faction's interests (Ch 1).

**Sanity Score** — optional 7th ability score for cosmic-horror campaigns (Ch 9).

**Sentient Item** — a magic item with its own INT/WIS/CHA, alignment, and (optionally) Special Purpose, run as an NPC (Ch 7).

**Session Zero** (implied) — the pre-campaign alignment on play style, naming conventions, and genre conventions referenced throughout Ch 1 and Ch 8.

**Special Purpose** (Sentient Item) — a goal (Aligned/Bane/Protector/Crusader/Templar/Destroyer/Glory Seeker/Lore Seeker/Destiny Seeker/Creator Seeker) a sentient item pursues (Ch 7).

**Ten Sentences** — the NPC-building framework: Occupation/History, Appearance, Abilities, Talent, Mannerism, Interactions, Useful Knowledge, Ideal, Bond, Flaw/Secret (Ch 4).

**Tiers of Play** — the four level bands (1-4, 5-10, 11-16, 17-20) calibrating threats, spell access, and magic item rarity (Ch 1).

**Typical DCs** — the fixed 5/10/15/20/25/30 difficulty ladder (Ch 8).

**Villain's Scheme** — the Objective (why) × Methods (how) two-axis villain generator (Ch 4).

**World-Shaking Event** — a campaign-structuring plot beat (~3 per campaign: beginning/middle/end) drawn from a d10 category table (Ch 1).

**XP Threshold** — a character's per-difficulty-tier (Easy/Medium/Hard/Deadly) XP value at their level, summed across the party to gauge encounter difficulty (Ch 3).
